Logo

An M and an E share one stem. The cobalt block is the E’s middle bar and doubles as a text cursor, a nod to design and code together. Use the full logo when space allows and the mark alone for avatars, favicons, and small spaces.

الشعار الكامل للمساحات الواسعة، والرمز وحده لصورة البروفايل والمساحات الصغيرة.

Do

  • ✓Leave clear space around the logo at least equal to the height of the mark.
  • ✓Minimum size: full logo 20px tall on screen, mark 16px.
  • ✓Use ink or ivory backgrounds, or a calm, dark photo.

Don’t

  • ✕Don’t stretch, rotate, or outline the logo.
  • ✕Don’t recolor the cobalt block or add shadows or gradients.
  • ✕Don’t place it on busy images or on cobalt; use the ivory version there.

Colors

Mostly ink and ivory. Cobalt is the spark: one accent per design (a button, a word, a line), never large areas.

القاعدة: الحبر والعاجي هما الأساس، والأزرق يُستخدم مرة واحدة فقط في كل تصميم كلمسة مميزة.

Suggested proportion: 70% ink · 20% ivory · 7% grays · 3% cobalt

Typography

All free on Google Fonts. Headlines use tight letter spacing in English (−3% to −4%) and normal spacing in Arabic. Never add letter spacing to Arabic; it breaks the joins between letters.

Voice

Confident, not loud

Short sentences. No exclamation marks, no “best in the market.” Let the work speak.

Clear, not technical

Talk about results for the client (trust, sales, inquiries), not code or tools.

Honest

Only real projects, real reviews, and real numbers. Never invent results.
